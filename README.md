# SQLite Database Query Documentation & Analytical Insights

This document describes the purpose, structure, and business value of the queries stored in `analytical_queries.sql`.

---

## 1. Vehicle Profitability & Margin Breakdown by Make & Model

* **Business Objective**: Identify which vehicle makes and models yield the highest total profit and strongest profit margins.
* **Key Metrics Calculated**:
  * `TotalUnitsSold`: Total number of vehicles sold per make/model.
  * `TotalRevenue`: Sum of sales prices from invoice lines.
  * `TotalCost`: Sum of purchasing cost price, spare parts, and labor costs.
  * `TotalProfit`: Net earnings calculated as revenue minus total combined costs.
  * `AverageProfitMarginPct`: Average percentage margin per sale.
* **Tables Involved**: `Stock`, `InvoiceLines`.

---

## 2. Client Segmentation & Regional Performance Analysis

* **Business Objective**: Evaluate client purchasing patterns across different organization sizes, types, and geographic locations to refine targeting and discount strategies.
* **Key Metrics Calculated**:
  * `UniqueClients`: Count of distinct clients within each category.
  * `TotalInvoices`: Volume of distinct orders/invoices generated.
  * `GrossSales`: Total sales revenue generated by group.
  * `AvgDiscountGiven` & `AvgDeliveryCharge`: Assessment of extra costs and price concessions.
* **Tables Involved**: `Clients`, `Countries`, `Invoices`, `InvoiceLines`.

---

## 3. Quarterly Revenue Trends & Seasonality Analytics

* **Business Objective**: Monitor financial progression over time, identify seasonal sales spikes, and observe discounting trends across quarters.
* **Key Metrics Calculated**:
  * `InvoiceCount`: Quarterly sales volume.
  * `QuarterlyRevenue`: Total revenue aggregate per quarter.
  * `AverageLineSalePrice`: Average ticket size per item line.
  * `TotalQuarterlyDiscounts`: Total value of discounts given in a quarter.
* **Tables Involved**: `Invoices`, `DateDimension`, `InvoiceLines`.

---

## 4. Inventory Efficiency: Vehicle Age & Mileage Impact on Profitability

* **Business Objective**: Determine how vehicle depreciation (age) and usage (mileage) affect ultimate profitability and sales price realization.
* **Key Metrics Calculated**:
  * `AgeGroup`: Categorization into `<3 Years`, `3-6 Years`, or `>6 Years`.
  * `MileageGroup`: Groupings into `Low (<30k)`, `Medium (30k-70k)`, or `High (>70k)`.
  * `AvgProfitPerUnit`: Average net earnings per vehicle sold within the tier.
* **Tables Involved**: `Stock`, `InvoiceLines`.
